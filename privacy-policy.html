Divine Pendulum: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Applies to Divine Pendulum for Windows (Microsoft Store), version 1.3 and later.
Publisher: Nareshkumar D

Summary

Divine Pendulum does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It works fully offline and has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Only your preferences stay on your own PC, in the app's private local data folder (for example: the chosen deity, pendulum size and position, swing and glow options, water-reminder settings and today's glass count). If something goes wrong, the app may also write a small error log to that folder. None of this is ever sent anywhere.

Pictures you add with "Add your own images…" stay in a folder on your PC. The app only reads them to show them on the pendulum. It never uploads or shares them.

When you uninstall the app, Windows deletes this local data folder.

Internet use

The app does not connect to the internet itself. Buying the app, the free trial and licensing are handled by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. The developer gets only the anonymous, aggregated sales and install reports that Microsoft Partner Center gives every developer, and never your name, e-mail or payment details.

Permissions

Like every desktop app, Divine Pendulum runs as a normal (full-trust) Windows program. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other personal information. "Run at Windows startup" is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
