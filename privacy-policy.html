Divine Pendulum: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 (updated for Android 1.0.2) · Applies to Divine Pendulum for Windows (Microsoft Store, version 1.3 and later) and Divine Pendulum for Android (Google Play, version 1.0 and later).
Publisher: Nareshkumar D

Summary

Divine Pendulum does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It works fully offline and has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The Android app does not even request the internet permission.

What the app stores, and where

Only your preferences stay on your own device, in the app's private storage (for example: the chosen deity, pendulum size and position, swing and glow options, water-reminder settings and today's glass count). On Windows the app may also write a small diagnostic log to that folder. None of this is ever sent anywhere.

Pictures you add yourself stay on your device and are only used to show them on the pendulum:

  • Windows: pictures you put in the folder opened by “Add your own images…”.
  • Android: a photo you pick with “Add your own photo”. Android's photo picker gives the app only the single picture you choose (the app has no access to the rest of your gallery). The app saves a framed copy in its private storage. It never uploads or shares it.

The Android app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data, so your settings and photos are not copied to Google servers. When you uninstall the app, Windows or Android deletes all of its data.

Internet use

The app does not connect to the internet itself. On Windows, buying the app, the free trial and licensing are handled by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. On Android, downloads and updates are handled by Google Play, under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer gets only the anonymous, aggregated install and sales reports that Microsoft Partner Center and Google Play Console give every developer, and never your name, e-mail or payment details.

Permissions

Windows

Like every desktop app, Divine Pendulum runs as a normal (full-trust) Windows program. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other personal information. “Run at Windows startup” is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

Android

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only to show the water reminder you switch on, and the small status notification of the floating pendulum.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): only if you start the “floating pendulum”, to draw the pendulum on top of other apps. The app cannot see, read or record what other apps show, and touches outside the pendulum go to your other apps as usual.
  • Foreground service: keeps the floating pendulum on screen while you use it; it stops when you tap “Stop” in its notification or in the app.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, only if you allow it) and full-screen notifications (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): only so the water reminder you switched on arrives on time while the phone is locked and can show a “Did you drink water?” screen with Yes / Later buttons over the lock screen. Vibration is used only for that reminder.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): only to set the next water reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer, no permission needed): only when you turn on “Tilt with phone”, to let the pendulum hang with gravity. Sensor readings are used instantly and never stored.

The Android app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, accounts, advertising ID or internet.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
